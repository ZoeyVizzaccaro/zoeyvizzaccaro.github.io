## This Train Ain't Big Enough For The Two Of Us
A game initially developed in a team of 3 for my Game Design I course in late 2025; it's a top-down 2D shooter developed in Unity where you are tasked with protecting a train and its cargo as it travels from town to town. You ride on the top of the train, shooting at progressively stronger bandits that are attempting to destroy the train. You earn cash for killing enemies and for getting to each town with as little damage done to the train as possible, and can use that cash to upgrade your weapons, hire vigilantes to help defend the train, and repair and improve your train cars. As lead developer, I created and expanded the idea for the game, implemented the enemies and gunplay, most of the menus and code, created a fair number of the sprites in the game, and even created the song used for the main gameplay loop. 
The game can be downloaded here: [https://zoeyism.itch.io/this-train-aint-big-enough-for-the-two-of-us](url)

## The Marbelous Ascent
A game developed in a team of 5 for my Game Design II course in early 2026; it's a high difficulty 3D platforming game developed in Unreal Engine 5, wherein the player controls a marble that can only move, jump, and brake, and must ascend to the top of the great dome. There are no separate levels, so if you fall, you will probably end up right back at the bottom and have to start over; there are a limited number of collectible checkpoints that the player can place wherever they want, but they are hidden behind even more difficult platforming challenges. As one of the programmers and level designers, I developed the braking mechanic and the speed boosters, fixed several bugs in the tilting platforms and finalized their functionality, performed general bug fixing where needed, and implemented around 2/3rds of the level and platforming challenges.
