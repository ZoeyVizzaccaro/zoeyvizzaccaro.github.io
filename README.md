## This Train Ain't Big Enough For The Two Of Us
A game initially developed in a team of 3 for my Game Design I class in late 2025; it's a top-down 2D shooter developed in Unity where you are tasked with protecting a train and its cargo as it travels from town to town. You ride on the top of the train, shooting at progressively stronger bandits that are attempting to destroy the train. You earn cash for killing enemies and for getting to each town with as little damage done to the train as possible, and can use that cash to upgrade your weapons, hire vigilantes to help defend the train, and repair and improve your train cars. As lead developer, I created and expanded the idea for the game, implemented the enemies and gunplay, most of the menus and code, created a fair number of the sprites in the game, and even created the song used for the main gameplay loop. The game can be downloaded here: [https://zoeyism.itch.io/this-train-aint-big-enough-for-the-two-of-us](url)
